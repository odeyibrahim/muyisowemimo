Our legacy

Informed by a standard

Muyi Sowemimo & Co. is an independent boutique practice built on one of Nigeria's most enduring legal lineages. Its principal, Muyiwa Sowemimo, trained at Fani-Kayode & Sowemimo (established 1970) — one of Nigeria's oldest indigenous law firms — and rose to Senior Counsel at its successor practice before establishing this firm.

That training gave him a discipline, not a nostalgia: rigorous preparation, institutional memory, and a clear understanding that in Nigeria, law does not operate in a vacuum. It operates alongside government, commerce and community.
